Frequently Asked Questions

Will the PDF match my Word document exactly?
Not necessarily. This tool turns DOCX content into a browser preview before printing. Basic text, headings and tables may carry over, but fonts, page breaks, headers, footers and complex layouts can change. Check the preview and the saved PDF before sharing it.
Are my documents uploaded anywhere?
No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your documents never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy and security.
Is .doc format supported?
Currently only .docx (Word 2007+) is supported. For older .doc files, please convert to .docx using Word or LibreOffice first.
How do I save as PDF?
Click "Print / Save as PDF", then select "Save as PDF" or "Microsoft Print to PDF" in the print dialog. This tool does not directly download a PDF; the available option depends on your browser and device.
Do images and tables display correctly?
Embedded images and basic tables usually display correctly. However, highly customized formatting (text wrapping around images, complex merged cells) may need manual adjustment.
